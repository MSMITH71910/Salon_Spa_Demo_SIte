FAQ

Membership Questions

Are there contracts?

No contracts — ever. All memberships are month-to-month and can be cancelled or paused at any time.

Can I use credits for any service?

Credits apply to any single service up to the equivalent value of your tier's base service price. Upgrades and add-ons are charged separately at your member rate.

Do credits roll over?

Credits roll over for one month only. We encourage you to use them — you deserve it!

Can I share my membership?

Memberships are individual and cannot be shared or transferred. However, members can purchase guest passes for friends at a 15% discount.

How do I upgrade or downgrade?

Simply call or text us at (555) 874-2190 and we'll adjust your plan starting next billing cycle — no hassle.
